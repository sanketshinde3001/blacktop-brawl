## Art and audio

The supplied game art was generated using Google Gemini and local Qwen-Image,
then keyed, trimmed, reviewed and edited for this project. Some animation frames
are derived from those images by the included scripts. Generation scripts may
require local reference caches that are deliberately not included in the repo;
playing and building use the supplied assets and require no API key or model.

Most sound effects are synthesized by the included DSP scripts. Music and engine
audio are synthesized at runtime. Existing generated clips include the win jingle,
punch, siren and Go voice line. Other voice lines use device speech synthesis;
voice availability and offline behaviour depend on the device and browser.

No extracted EA game files are included. This provenance note is not a guarantee
that generated content has no resemblance to third-party material. Report any
specific asset concern through a GitHub issue so it can be reviewed and replaced.
