06 / Before you ride

Game questions.

Everything you need to get on the road.

What is Blacktop Brawl?

Blacktop Brawl is a free, independent motorcycle combat racing game inspired by Road Rash. It combines retro racing, punches, kicks, weapons, traffic, police, and bike upgrades. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts.

Is Blacktop Brawl free, and do I need a download or account?

The game is free to play in your browser. No download or account is required. Open the game, choose a mode, and start racing.

How do I play and progress through the career?

Use the arrow keys to accelerate, brake, and steer. Press A or D to hit, S to kick, and Space for nitro. Hold Q to look behind, press E to change camera, and Esc to pause. Finish in fourth place or better to qualify and spend race winnings on bikes and upgrades.

How many levels, roads, and bikes are there?

The career has five levels across five roads: Sierra Nevada, Pacific Coast, Redwood Forest, Palm Desert, and Grass Valley. The garage has 12 bikes, with engine, tyre, brake, armour, and nitro upgrades.

Can I play on a phone or with a gamepad?

Blacktop Brawl supports keyboard, gamepad, and touch controls. On phones and tablets, use landscape orientation for the on-screen controls. The game runs in a web browser and requires JavaScript.

Does it have multiplayer?

Yes. Two-player split screen lets two people race on the same device using separate keyboard controls or gamepads. It is local multiplayer, not online matchmaking. Career, quick race, endless, and daily challenge modes are also available.

Where is my progress saved?

Career progress, options, records, and achievements are saved in browser storage on your device. Clearing site data or using another browser or device may remove or separate your progress. There is no account-based cloud save.
